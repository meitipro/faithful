## FAQ

### Which languages?

Twenty, listed in [Languages and limits](/docs/more/languages-and-limits). Models are stronger in widely spoken
languages, so Faithful is most reliable there; pair a rare language with a human spot check.

### Can I use machine translation?

Yes. The verdict is about the result. Raw machine output is approved only if it is faithful and reads naturally; the
held-out Turkish case in the [evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation), correct but stiff, came back FLAWED for the
stiffness.

### What about style?

Style choices that keep the meaning are accepted. Faithful does not enforce house style; required renderings go in the
[glossary](/docs/concepts/glossaries).

### Can a translator talk the judge into approving?

An instruction inside a translation is text that is not in the source, which is an addition, which is FLAWED. Every
text is also fenced, so it cannot close its block and pose as the contract's own words. The evaluation has that case;
it came back FLAWED.

### Who pays the fees?

Studio Next takes a small fee deposit on every write, from the SDK estimate, and refunds most of it when the
transaction is final. Each person pays for their own writes. Test GEN is free from the site's faucet.

### Why is my claim still shown as claimed after 48 hours?

The contract has no clock of its own: an expired claim is released by the next claim or close that touches it. The
site shows it as open as soon as it expires, and anyone may claim it.

### Which network?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. See [Addresses and network](/docs/reference/addresses).
