title: Programs, sections and claims
description: How a program holds money and sections, and how one claim locks one section in one language.
---

## Programs

A program belongs to the account that opened it, its **maintainer**. It fixes a source language, one to eight target
languages, a glossary and a **rate** per section per language. Only the maintainer adds sections and closes the
program. Anyone may top up its pool; the funding log records every payment and who sent it.

## Sections

A section is at most 2,500 characters of source text with a title. Its id is global across programs, so a section's
page is `/s/<section>/<lang>`. A section has one state per target language:

| On chain | On the board | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN | Open | Anyone may claim it |
| CLAIMED | Claimed | A translator holds it; their claim expires 48 hours after it began |
| CLAIMED, last submission refused by the exact checks | Pre-check | The holder is fixing a difference |
| CLAIMED, after one FLAWED verdict | Flawed | The holder is on their one revision |
| JUDGING | Judging | A submission passed the exact checks and waits for `judge` |
| APPROVED | OK | Judged FAITHFUL, paid, and in the export |

States are always words, never colour alone. Blue marks approved sections only.

## Claims

- `claim(section_id, lang)` locks one section in one language for 48 hours and reserves one rate from the pool.
- A translator holds at most three live claims at once.
- A claim that is not submitted in time reopens the section. The contract has no clock of its own, so an expired
  claim is released by the next claim or close that touches it; the site already shows it as open.
- A submission waiting for the judge never expires. Anyone can call `judge` on it, so a translator who stops after
  submitting cannot block the maintainer from closing.

## Who may do what

Every write is bound to an address, and a test over the contract source fails if a new write is not:

| Write | Who |
|---|---|
| `create_program` | anyone; they become the maintainer |
| `fund` | anyone; recorded in the funding log |
| `add_sections`, `close` | the maintainer only |
| `claim` | anyone, for themselves |
| `submit` | only the holder of the claim |
| `judge` | anyone; the verdict is the validators', and `judged_by` records who asked |
| `withdraw` | anyone, for their own balance |
